Momentum — the 12-1 construction: return from twelve months ago to one month ago. The skipped month (1M ⚠) mean-reverts, which is exactly why it is excluded from the formation window and shown separately.

Value — rank average of EBITDA yield (1÷EV/EBITDA), free-cash-flow yield, and earnings yield. Multi-ratio on purpose: a low P/E alone walks into value traps. Banks legitimately report no EBITDA or FCF and are scored on what they have.

Quality — rank average of ROE, ROA (the available ROIC proxy), operating margin, and low leverage. The trap flag marks names in the cheap third on value and the bottom third on quality — cheap-because-dying. reversal marks a last month that fought the 12-1 signal.
